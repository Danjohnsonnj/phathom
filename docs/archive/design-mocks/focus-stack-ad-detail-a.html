The Intent Debt

Jun 8, 2026 at 11:12 PM

Technical debt lives in your code. Cognitive debt lives in your head. After years of building things myself and watching other builders, I've started noticing a new third layer: intent debt, the pile of things you meant to do properly "just not yet", which compounds quietly until every shortcut becomes the default.
